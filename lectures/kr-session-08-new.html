Further study

Four directions the field is moving in, each touching a layer you built

SHACL 1.2

A W3C Working Draft (September 2026). Watch what it adds to the shapes you wrote, as it moves toward a Recommendation.

The RML Working Group

Mappings for CSV, JSON and XML becoming a W3C standard; its charter was being refined in 2026.

Time and space

Facts that change over time, and places: what this course left out. GeoSPARQL covers the spatial side.

Neurosymbolic work

Models that respect rules by construction, not by being checked afterwards as ours were.

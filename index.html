Knowledge Representation

Each lecture is one HTML page. It runs in any browser with no install, keeps your place and your answers on your own device, expands into a self-study version, and prints as a handout with the instructor's notes attached.

The eight sessions

24 contact hours · 8 sessions of 3 hours · MSc. One enterprise problem, a supply chain, carried from raw operational data through to a deployed system that can be queried in natural language. Every lab is done individually on the course's own case, for understanding, not for a grade; teams of 2 to 3 apply the same techniques to their own chosen topic and database, evaluated at the end.

The labs

Each session has a hands-on lab with real code on the course's supply chain data. Start with the setup guide (Python, Docker), then open the lab's README for its steps and the answers to expect. Each README is on GitHub.

How to use a lecture

Presenting

Open the lecture, press F for fullscreen and P for presenter view on your second screen. T starts the session timer; the pacing readout tells you whether you are behind the plan.

Studying

Press S. Every definition becomes an inline note, every held-back answer opens, and your position and answers are remembered on your own device. / searches the whole lecture.

Offline and on paper

Install the site from your browser's address bar and lectures you have opened work without a network. Handout in the bottom bar prints one page per slide with the notes attached.
